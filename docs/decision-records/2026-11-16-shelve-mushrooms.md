# Decision: shelve mushroom exploration

Date: 2026-11-16. Decided by: project owner. Status: shelved (not rejected permanently).

## Context
Indoor specialty mushrooms were evaluated as a vertical at one room, ten containers, and
three high-density containers (`knowledge/verticals/mushrooms/`).

## Findings
- One room: base NPV −$46k; pays $7–25 per labor hour; a job, not a capital return.
- Ten containers: −$1.03M; three dense containers: −$0.90M. Volume exceeds the Wichita
  specialty market, so price falls to distributor level while labor stays manual.
- A Haysville grower already supplies Wichita chefs and GreenAcres.

## Decision
Shelve. Keep the files. Revisit only if a family member wants a year-round indoor job or
a buyer commits to 500+ lb/week at a premium price.
