# Pitch to Tony

Status: draft, 2026-11-16. Tony manages the farming operation and has first say on
anything that happens on the farm. The person pitching is a future trust member who is not
a main decision maker and is offering AI and technology help; plans go to the other
stakeholders only with Tony's approval.

## Purpose of the conversation
Not to sell a plan. To learn what Tony is carrying that is not physical work, and to show
that this project can take some of it. The farm plan is only as good as the knowledge of
the person running the ground, and nothing here replaces that.

## Opening (two minutes)
You run this farm and you have the first say on everything that happens on it; nothing
here changes that. I have been building a planning tool on the side, using AI and the
public data that is out there, to see if it can make the farm more profitable or find
revenue the trust is leaving on the table. It is still preparation, nothing is set up or
committed. Here is what it does: it tracks input prices and the
world events that move them, models which crops pay on which fields, keeps the soil test
and water picture per field, watches the laws and programs that affect us, and costs out
side businesses before anyone spends a dollar. It is a set of files and tools, and it
answers questions the same way every time. It does not drive a tractor. Where it could
earn its keep is the office side: the buying, the marketing, the paperwork, the planning,
the watching.

## The question that matters
**Outside of the physical labor, what do you need help with?**
Let him answer fully before anything else. Follow-ups only if he stalls:
- What eats your evenings in winter? In season?
- What decision each year do you least like making, or make last?
- When did a price, a rule, a deadline or a buyer catch you off guard?
- What paperwork do you put off: insurance, FSA, water reports, pesticide records, taxes?
- What do you wish you knew before you bought inputs each year?
- Who do you call when you need to sell something that is not wheat or milo?
- Is there anything you have wanted to try on the farm but never had time to work up?
- What should this project leave alone?

## What we can offer, matched to likely answers
| If he says | We have |
|---|---|
| Buying fertilizer and fuel at the wrong time | the early-warning system and weekly buy, hold or defer signals |
| Too much paperwork, deadlines slip | the compliance checklist and program calendar; the policy watch |
| Not sure which crop pays on which field | the agronomist and water agents, soil-test driven field plan |
| Marketing beyond the elevator | the buyer catalog and network plan |
| Wants to try something new but no time to cost it | seven costed business plans with spreadsheets, and a template for any other idea |
| Machinery costs and software lock-in | equipment cost framework; open-source guidance and repair-rights notes |
| The trust makes things complicated | the trust constraints framework and an operating structure to review with counsel |

## What we need from him
- Field list, soil tests, crop history, equipment list, suppliers, buyers he already knows.
- His read on what is realistic for labor and time.
- Permission to be wrong in front of him; the files are drafts until he corrects them.

## Close
Ask which one thing from his list he would want handled first, and agree on when to show
him the first result. Make clear the sequence: nothing goes to the other stakeholders
until he has approved it, and he can stop any part of it. Record his answers in `knowledge/operations/farm-profile.md` under
stakeholders and in `docs/decision-records/`.
