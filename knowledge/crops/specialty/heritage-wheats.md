# Heritage and ancient wheats

> Research note (2026-11-23): fetch to NASS/K-State PDFs was blocked; figures come from
> search summaries. No farm-gate or contract price for Turkey Red, spelt, emmer or einkorn
> was found anywhere; all raw prices below are **verify**. Net per acre = gross revenue
> minus direct costs, excluding land and machinery ownership.

## 1. Fit for this farm
- **Turkey Red (hard red winter, landrace introduced to Kansas by Mennonites, 1874).**
  Agronomically it fits exactly where modern HRW fits: same drill, sprayer, combine, bins,
  planting window (see `knowledge/crops/wheat-hard-red-winter.md`). It is tall and weak-
  strawed, so it lodges under modern N rates; it lacks modern disease resistance (stripe
  and leaf rust, Hessian fly) and yields roughly a third to half of modern varieties.
  Seed is public (no PVP), so the farm can save and sell it.
- **Spelt, emmer, einkorn (hulled wheats).** Winter types exist for spelt and some emmer;
  einkorn is mostly spring-sown in the north. All are untested in south-central Kansas
  heat at grain fill (**verify** with K-State; no Kansas trial found). They harvest in the
  hull with the existing combine, but the farm lacks a dehuller and an aspirator; hulled
  grain has no local buyer.
- **Rotation.** Same as wheat; same volunteer and disease bridge issues. Keep identity
  preserved and away from commodity HRW in bins (Turkey Red mixed into commodity wheat is
  a loss; commodity wheat mixed into Turkey Red ruins the story).
- **Trust and labor.** Growing is agriculture. Flour, berries and seed packaging go to the
  LLC. Seed sales of bulk grain can sit with the farm if the farm holds the seed
  registration (**verify** with counsel which entity should hold it).
- **Commitment.** Annual.

## 2. Agronomy summary
- **Planting.** Turkey Red: same window as HRW (~Oct 1–20 for Sedgwick County per the
  wheat file), 60–75 lb/ac. Spelt: similar winter window, higher seeding rate by weight
  because of hulls (~100–120 lb/ac hulled, **verify**).
- **Fertility.** Turkey Red: 40–60 lb N/ac at most (lodging); P and K by soil test.
  Spelt/emmer similar or lower.
- **Pests and disease.** Expect rust and Hessian fly losses; fungicide may be needed in
  wet springs; plant after the Hessian fly-free date. Turkey Red's drought tolerance is a
  marketing claim, not a trial result.
- **Harvest.** Turkey Red: normal wheat harvest in June; lower cylinder speed in lodged
  stands. Hulled wheats: combine settings that leave the hull on (lower speed, wider
  concave); bulk density is low, so bins fill fast.
- **Cleaning and storage.** Air-screen clean; test protein, falling number, DON;
  dedicated bin or totes.
- **Seed source.** Organic Turkey Red seed is offered by Albert Lea Seed, Wood Prairie and
  others; start with 1–2 acres and build seed.

**Comparison to dryland sorghum (~$100/ac base, `grain-sorghum.md`) and modern HRW
(~$107/ac: 50 bu x $6.45 - ~$215).** Raw Turkey Red at $56/ac loses to both even with a
$3 premium; it pays only through a finished product.

## 4. Raw-crop market
- No elevator differentiates Turkey Red. Possible buyers: Kansas craft mills (Heartland
  Mill, Marienthal; Farmer Direct Foods, New Cambria; see the wheat-flour competitor
  table), Kansas distilleries seeking a Kansas-heritage mash bill (spirits plan Route A),
  home-milling retailers, and seed buyers (gardeners, heritage grain growers).
- Volume ceiling: small; probably 500–1,500 bu/yr across all raw buyers (**verify** by
  outreach).
- Competitors: organic Turkey Red from Kansas and Midwest growers already online (Fair
  Plains Grains, Heritage Grains Co, Breadtopia).

## 5. Finished products
Default structure: the LLC buys Turkey Red from the trust farm at $9.45/bu ($236/ac base)
and processes off trust land. FAMILY $11/hr; HIRED $18/hr sensitivity. Uplift = revenue -
variable - labor - raw at market value - fixed share, per acre processed.

**H1. Stone-ground Turkey Red flour (through the wheat-flour vertical).**
Do not build a separate mill: run Turkey Red as a premium SKU through the mill planned in
`knowledge/verticals/wheat-flour/business-plan.md` (stage-1 mill line $110,000 there).
Incremental capital ~$5,000 (dedicated bin/totes, labels). 1,500 lb grain -> ~1,280 lb
flour/ac. Price $2.00/lb blended (heritage premium over the vertical's $1.50 base;
**verify** with bakers). Variable $0.20/lb excluding labor. Labor 13 hr/acre-eq. Fixed
$1,000/yr over 10 acres. Uplift **$1,825/ac** ($1,734 at HIRED). Ceiling ~20 acres
(~25,000 lb/yr to artisan bakers, pizzerias, retail). Regulatory: the flour LLC's KDA food
processor license. Competitors: Heartland Mill, Farmer Direct Foods, out-of-state heritage
mills. Bakery partnerships ("Turkey Red loaf" with a Wichita bakery) are the sales channel
for this product, not a separate product. Verdict: **pursue**, only if and when the
flour vertical runs.

**H2. Turkey Red wheat berries, 5-lb bag (home millers, sprouting).**
Capital ~$8,000 (air-screen cleaner shared with the birdseed vertical, scale, sealer).
285 bags/ac at $10 ($2.00/lb; retail organic berries online $8–44 by size). Variable
$2.35/bag (bag, label, testing, shipping and platform share). Labor 23 hr/acre-eq. Fixed
$1,500/yr over 5 acres. Uplift **$1,391/ac**. Ceiling ~8 acres (online competition is
established and organic). Regulatory: KDA license or DTC exemption (**verify**). Verdict:
**test** alongside H1.

**H3. Turkey Red seed (bulk 50-lb and garden packets).**
Capital ~$2,000 (shared cleaner; packets). 1,350 lb clean seed/ac at $0.80/lb blended
(organic seed ~$43 per 50 lb from one seller; conventional must sell lower). Variable
~$0.19/lb (germination tests, bags, shipping). Labor 15 hr. Fixed $300/yr (seed
registration). Uplift **$327/ac**. Ceiling ~3 acres. Regulatory: KDA seed registration,
germination test within 9 months, variety named on the label (no VNS). Verdict: **test**
(low cost; builds the seed stock needed for H1–H2 anyway).

**H4. Dehulled ancient-grain berries (spelt, emmer/farro, einkorn).**
Capital ~$25,000 (impact or stone dehuller plus aspirator; **verify**; small units cost
15–19 c/lb to run, eOrganic). 2,000 lb hulled -> 1,200 lb clean berries/ac at $3.00/lb
(einkorn retail $2.50–5.00/lb). Variable $0.65/lb (dehulling, bags, fees). Labor 24 hr.
Fixed $4,600/yr over 5 acres. Uplift **$1,436/ac**. Ceiling ~5 acres. Regulatory: KDA
license; wheat allergen labeling (none of these is safe for celiacs). Competitors: Jovial
(einkorn), Bluebird Grain Farms, many online. Verdict: **test** on 1 acre of winter spelt
first, using toll dehulling if one can be found; no capital until the crop survives a
Kansas summer.

Screened out: dried pasta and crackers (extruder, dryer, co-packer minimums; better as a
flour sale to a Wichita pasta maker or bakery) — **skip** as an owned product.

## 6. Risks and disqualifiers
- **Top disqualifier: yield.** At ~25 bu/ac Turkey Red forfeits ~$170/ac of modern HRW
  revenue at $6.45 (and more at the 2026 $7–9 cash prices); the product premium must
  carry it every year.
- No farm-gate price exists; every raw price is an assumption.
- Lodging and rust; no modern resistance.
- Identity-preservation errors in shared bins.
- Hulled wheats untested in Kansas heat; dehulling capital with no local toll option.
- Market already served online by organic competitors; the farm is conventional.

## 7. Decision rules
1. Plant Turkey Red only on acres matched to a flour, berry or seed demand estimate with
   letters of intent for 60% of output.
2. Start with 2–5 acres; scale only if realized yield is at least 22 bu/ac over two years.
3. No dehuller purchase until a 1-acre spelt trial yields at least 1,800 lb/ac hulled and
   a buyer has tested the berries.
4. Price every LLC transfer at $6.45 HRW base plus the documented premium on the sale date.
