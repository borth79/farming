# <Crop>

## 1. Fit for this farm
Climate, soil, water, season, rotation fit with wheat/sorghum/soybeans, equipment the farm
already has (tractors, grain combine, planters, sprayers) versus what it lacks; herbicide
carryover conflicts; trust and labor fit; perennial vs annual commitment.

## 2. Agronomy summary
Varieties for Kansas, planting window and method, seeding rate, fertility, key pests and
diseases, harvest method and timing, drying, cleaning, storage.

## 3. Yield and raw-crop economics
Yield per acre base/adverse/favorable with Kansas or Plains sources; raw-crop price and
buyers (contract or spot) with dates; direct cost per acre; net per acre; comparison to
dryland sorghum on the same acre (net per acre from knowledge/crops/grain-sorghum.md).

## 4. Raw-crop market
Who buys it within reach of Sedgwick County, contract terms, volume ceilings,
competitors, price history and volatility.
