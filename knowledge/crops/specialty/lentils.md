# Lentils

## 1. Fit for this farm
- **Unsuitable.** Lentil is a cool-season, low-statured crop of the Palouse, Montana, North
  Dakota and Saskatchewan. It needs a cool, dry flowering and pod-fill period.
- **Climate**: a spring planting at Wichita flowers in May–June heat and humidity. Expect
  heat-limited pod set and Ascochyta, anthracnose and Botrytis. Field peas, which are more
  heat-tolerant, failed for heat at K-State's Garden City site.
- **Equipment**: lentil pods sit 2–4 in off the ground, so harvest needs a flex draper set
  at ground level, often after land rolling at planting. The farm's equipment is set up for
  row crops.
- **Season**: harvest would fall in late June to July, on top of wheat harvest.
- **Market**: no buyer within reach.
- **Weeds**: poor competitor. Few herbicides. Atrazine plant-back.

## 2. Agronomy summary
Not developed. If ever trialed: small red or green types; inoculate with pea/vetch
Rhizobium; seed about 60–80 lb/ac in early March; PRE weed control only; desiccate and
straight-cut.

harvested area fell to 585,000 ac in 2026.

## 5. Finished products
Lentil products can be made from bought lentils at least as well as from farm lentils, so
none justifies growing them. Each is shown briefly for completeness. Economics are per
acre-equivalent of 540 lb cleaned, raw charged at $96, FAMILY labor. Uplift follows the
schema.

- **Bagged lentils, 1–2 lb**: $4.00/lb blended (estimate); capital $8,000 (shared); 27 hr;
  LLC net $308; uplift $342. **Skip**, because there is no local-origin advantage worth the
  agronomic risk.
- **Lentil component of soup mix**: for the bean soup mix (`dry-beans.md` 5b), buy lentils.
  LLC net -$664 as a standalone product. **Skip.**
- **Sprouting lentils**: retail sprouting-seed niche. FDA Subpart M expectations and
  liability on tiny volume. LLC net -$3,116. **Skip.**

## 6. Risks and disqualifiers
- **Top disqualifier**: climate. Heat and humidity at flowering and fill.
- Ground-level harvest with equipment the farm lacks.
- Harvest timing clashes with wheat.
- No market within reach.

## 7. Decision rules
1. Do not grow lentils. Re-examine only if heat-tolerant varieties show 1,200+ lb/ac in
   southern Plains trials.
