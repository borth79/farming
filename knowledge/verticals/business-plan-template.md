# <Vertical name>

## 1. Concept
What is made, from which crop, for whom, and why this farm can do it.

## 2. Product line
Products, grades, pack sizes, shelf life, certifications or claims.

## 3. Market
Target customers (wholesale and retail), market size within reach (Wichita metro and
region), competitors, pricing benchmarks with sources and dates, channel strategy, how
first accounts are won, sales volume ramp by year.

## 4. Raw material and agronomy link
Acres required by stage, variety or hybrid requirements, quality specs (protein, oil,
moisture, mycotoxin), identity preservation, storage, what happens to off-spec grain.

## 5. Process and equipment
Flow from field to finished product. Equipment list with capacity, price range, new vs
used, supplier examples, utilities. Automation plan and labor hours per unit output.
Facility needs (space, floor, power, pest control, food-safety design).

## 6. Regulatory and food safety
Licenses, registrations, labeling, allergen handling, testing, recordkeeping, insurance.

## 7. Operations plan
Staffing, production schedule, logistics and delivery, packaging, inventory.

## 8. Financials (summary; detail lives in assumptions.csv and the model)
Capital by stage, operating cost per unit, price per unit, gross margin, breakeven
volume, 5-year cash flow summary for base / adverse / favorable, payback, return on
capital, comparison with the same acres sold as grain.

## 9. Staged growth plan
Stage 1 pilot, stage 2 scale, stage 3 expansion: triggers to move between stages,
capital at each, acres at each.

## 10. Risks and mitigations
Market, operational, food safety, weather and crop, regulatory, key-person.

## 11. Decision rules
Go / no-go criteria, kill criteria, review dates.

## 12. Open items
What must be verified before investing a dollar.
