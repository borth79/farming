# Verticals summary (market-checked, 2026-10-31)

Base cases below use sales and price assumptions corrected by each vertical's
competitor analysis. Favorable cases are upper bounds and must not size debt. Adverse
cases stack every bad factor and are stress tests. All inputs carry "verify" flags.

| Vertical | Stage 1 capital | Base NPV @8% | Base payback | Breakeven acres | Score /5 | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Sorghum flour + popped | $72k | +$24k | year 5 | 5 | 3.0 | conditional go on sales-gated pilot; time to cash is the weakness |
| Wheat flour | $110k | −$43k | none in 5 yr | 14 | 3.2 | marginal without bakery stage; pursue only through stage-0 market test |
| Sunflower oil | $70k (after $19k birdseed stage) | −$100k | none in 5 yr | 38 | 2.6 | only line with real upside but capital and slow cash drag it; restaurant price test first |
| Birdseed only | $19k | −$36k | none | 126 | 3.0 | learning stage at a loss; justified only as entry to oil |

## What changed from the first-pass models
- Wheat: year-5 base acres cut 50 → 40 and sell-through 90 → 75 % (Wichita specialty pool ~300k lb/yr; prior plan took 28 %).
- Sorghum: sell-through cut to 25/35/45/55/60 % (Wichita sorghum-flour pool ~45k lb/yr; shelf ceiling 4–10k lb for a new brand).
- Oil: blend price $4.75 → $4.25/lb; years 3–5 sell-through capped 80–90 % (Wichita premium oil pool ~8–15k gal/yr; regional distribution needed beyond).
- Birdseed: price $0.50 → $0.46/lb; bulk floor $0.195 → $0.21/lb (AMS Jamestown KS bid 2026-10-23).

## Common disqualifiers
- Letters of intent below the stated floor volume and price after sampling ~20 accounts.
- Licensed or allergen-free space cannot be built under the stated cap.
- An established competitor already serving Wichita at or below the plan's price.
- Crop quality (protein, mycotoxin, popping yield, oil recovery) fails spec two seasons.

## Order of attack
1. Sorghum flour pilot (highest score on economics, lowest breakeven acres) — gated on
   5 LOIs ≥ 8,000 lb/yr at $2.25/lb.
2. Wheat flour stage-0 market test (<$1,000) in parallel; bakery stage is where the value is.
3. Sunflower: restaurant oil test with toll-pressed oil before any press; birdseed only
   as a cheap learning stage.
