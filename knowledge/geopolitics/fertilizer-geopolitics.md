# Fertilizer geopolitics

## Summary
Fertilizer supply is more concentrated than grain supply. A handful of countries and
companies control potash, phosphate, and a large share of exported urea. Policy
decisions (export bans, quotas, sanctions, tariffs) move US prices within weeks. This
file maps each exposure to this farm's cost lines and defines the indicators that
trigger procurement actions.

## Exposure map
| Nutrient | Concentration | Key actors | Transmission to Kansas price | Lead time |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Nitrogen (urea, UAN, ammonia) | moderate; natural gas is the choke point | Russia, Middle East (Qatar, Saudi, Iran), China export policy, Trinidad, US producers | NOLA import parity; gas price pass-through | 2–8 weeks |
| Phosphate (DAP/MAP) | high | Morocco (OCP), China, Russia, US (Mosaic); countervailing duties on Moroccan and Russian imports | NOLA barge; duty changes | 4–12 weeks |
| Potash | very high | Canada, Russia, Belarus; sanctions on Belarus | contract prices, Canadian rail | 1–6 months |
| Sulfur / ammonia inputs to P | moderate | refinery and gas output | embedded in DAP/MAP | months |

## Scenario register (probabilities set in the weekly scan)
| Scenario | Trigger indicators | Expected impact | Pre-committed response |
|---|---|---|---|
| Russian/Belarus sanctions tightened on potash or urea | OFAC/EU announcements, shipping insurance withdrawal | potash and urea up sharply within weeks | buy ahead on K if soil tests need it; accelerate N layering |
| China restricts urea or phosphate exports | customs inspection notices, quota news | urea and DAP up globally | advance purchase of next-season P; raise N layer |
| European gas spike curtails ammonia production | TTF above threshold, plant closure news | global ammonia and urea up | buy N forward |
| Middle East conflict / Hormuz disruption | shipping alerts, insurance rates | urea, ammonia, and diesel up | fill N and fuel storage immediately |
| US countervailing duty changes on phosphate | ITC/Commerce rulings | DAP/MAP basis shift | adjust P timing |
| Gulf hurricane / Mississippi low water | NOAA, Corps of Engineers | import logistics; Plains basis up | pull purchases forward in Aug–Oct |
| Easing scenario: new capacity, peace, sanctions lifted | announcements | prices soften | slow layering, defer |

## Indicators to monitor weekly
- Sanctions announcements and shipping insurance changes.
- Chinese customs and export-quota notices.
- TTF and Henry Hub gas prices versus thresholds in the energy file.
- India urea tender results (price and volume).
- Baltic and Gulf freight rates.
- Mississippi River stage at Memphis and St. Louis in late summer.
