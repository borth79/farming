# Units and conversions

## The bushel
A bushel is a volume unit (1.2445 cubic feet, 35.239 liters), but grain is weighed, not
measured, and converted to bushels at a standard weight per bushel for each crop. In
practice a bushel is a fixed number of pounds.

| Crop | lb per bushel | bu per metric ton | bu per short ton | Standard moisture | Traded unit |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Wheat (HRW) | 60 | 36.744 | 33.333 | 13.5% | bushel |
| Soybeans | 60 | 36.744 | 33.333 | 13.0% | bushel |
| Corn | 56 | 39.368 | 35.714 | 15.5% | bushel |
| Grain sorghum | 56 | 39.368 | 35.714 | 14.0% | bushel (also cwt in some reports) |
| Sunflower | n/a (test weight 25–32) | 22.046 cwt/t | 20 cwt/t | 10.0% | hundredweight (cwt = 100 lb) |
| Oats | 32 | 68.894 | 62.5 | 14.0% | bushel |
| Barley | 48 | 45.929 | 41.667 | 14.5% | bushel |
| Alfalfa hay | — | 1.1023 short ton per metric ton | — | ~15% moisture for storage | short ton |

## Scale ticket to paid bushels
1. Net lb = gross weight − tare (empty truck).
2. Gross bu = net lb ÷ standard lb/bu.
3. Moisture shrink: paid bu = gross bu × (100 − delivered moisture) ÷ (100 − standard
   moisture), often with an added handling shrink factor (commonly 1.3–1.4 for corn,
   buyer-specific; verify at each elevator).
4. Apply discounts ($/bu) for test weight below standard, damage, foreign material,
   sprout, aflatoxin, etc., per the buyer's schedule.

## Test weight
Test weight is the actual lb per volume bushel of the delivered grain, a quality measure.
Payment still uses standard lb/bu from the scale; low test weight is penalized through
the discount schedule. US No. 1 minimums: wheat 60, soybeans 56, corn 56, sorghum 57.

## Yield conversions
- bu/acre × lb/bu ÷ 2,204.6 × 2.471 = metric tons per hectare.
- 1 t/ha wheat ≈ 14.87 bu/acre; corn ≈ 15.93 bu/acre.

## Logistics rules of thumb
| Item | Figure |
|---|---|
| Hopper-bottom semi (legal GVW ~80,000 lb) | ~950–1,000 bu wheat or soybeans; ~1,000–1,050 bu corn or sorghum (verify local limits) |
| Tandem grain truck | ~400–450 bu |
| Grain cart | 500–1,500 bu depending on model |
| Bin capacity from dimensions | bu ≈ 0.6283 × diameter(ft)² × eave height(ft) for the cylinder, plus cone |
